# BaSa3D Vision

## Mission
Make it easy to discover, buy, customize, and order 3D-printed products.

## Initial business modes
1. Ready-made products with fixed prices.
2. Custom 3D-print requests that require review/quote.

## MVP success
A real customer can browse products, place an order, and the owner can accurately see what was ordered, what stock moved, and what needs to be produced/shipped.
